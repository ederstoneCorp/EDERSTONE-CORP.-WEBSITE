Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

EderStone Corp respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us about our services. This page explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may receive the contact details and project information you choose to share. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and discuss potential work.

Website usage

This website is a company information and project-enquiry site. It does not currently provide user accounts or a built-in payment service. Our hosting provider and embedded third-party services may process technical information needed to deliver their services, subject to their own policies.

Cookies and third-party services

Fonts and links to external services may connect your browser to third-party websites. Those providers may process technical data according to their own privacy policies. This site does not intentionally use advertising trackers.

How information is used

  • To reply to messages and project enquiries.
  • To understand and scope requested services.
  • To maintain, protect and improve the website.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where required.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell contact information. Information may be handled by service providers needed to operate our communications or website, or disclosed where legally required. We retain enquiries only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, business records and applicable obligations.

Your choices

You can choose what information to include when contacting us. For questions about information you have shared, email ederstonecorp.dev@gmail.com.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as our website and services evolve. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

A few useful details before we start a conversation.

What kind of projects do you take on?

We focus on websites, web applications, Android apps, software systems, data analysis and AI-enabled solutions. Share your goal and we can discuss the best direction.

Can you work from an idea rather than a complete specification?

Yes. We can begin by clarifying the problem, identifying the essential features and outlining a practical first version.

How do I request a quote?

Send a short description of your project, its intended users, key features and preferred timeline by email or WhatsApp. We can then discuss scope and next steps.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Digital project discussions and collaboration can be handled remotely. Contact us to discuss the needs and working arrangements for your project.
